# Highlights

_Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence submission. Each highlight <= 85 characters._

- Excess fuel is attributed to driver vs vehicle by physically distinct signatures
- A kinematic axis flags driver excess; a combustion axis flags rich-mixture faults
- Multi-source method learns each cause signature where no joint labels exist
- Frozen source signatures attribute cause at 89% where fuel magnitude is at chance
- Validated on a real fleet, an engine-fault bench, and two driving datasets
